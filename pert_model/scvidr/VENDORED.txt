notes: VAE with explicit single-/multi-dose modes. `VIDR.predict(continuous=True,
       doses=[...])` interpolates the latent perturbation with
       delta * log1p(d) / log1p(max(doses)), so arbitrary doses are supported.
       Gene space is the AnnData gene space (the published liver datasets use
       ~5,000 genes). Hard dependency: scvi-tools 0.13.0 / PyTorch 1.8.
