# Compare Tahoe pretraining and tenv fine-tuning across the Step 03 models

**Status:** proposed (2026-11-09). The comparison, training and evaluation rules are agreed. The per-model dose interfaces and control handling are under design review.

`stage2/07_models_transfer/` compares PRnet, CPA, chemCPA, CMonge and PerturbDiff, the five learned entrants from [Step 03](0042-tenv-model-benchmark-redesign.md). For each model and seed, compare the Tahoe-pretrained predictor with its tenv-fine-tuned descendant. Use the same frozen [Step 05 cold-cm-drug fold 0](0047-perturbdiff-tahoe-transfer-to-tenv.md) for all models at seeds `17`, `42` and `2026`. Tahoe can contain drug blocks held out in tenv under this exposure policy.

Use the Step 03 Morgan representation with a jointly trained, model-specific linear chemical adapter. Use the [Step 06 continuous log dose](0049-perturbdiff-transfer-optimization-comparisons.md) convention in both stages. For positive doses, apply an affine projection to `log10(d / 0.05 µM)`. The common scale lets the source dose encoder carry over to tenv and evaluates positive doses directly, including 10 µM. Step 06 gives PerturbDiff a separate trainable zero-dose vector. The dose integration and control paths of the other four models remain open.

All five models share the Tahoe fold 0 training and validation memberships used by the Step 06 `log` arm and the ordered 18,164-gene CM axis. Select each stage's checkpoint by its own model-specific validation objective on that stage's validation set. Reuse the Step 06 `log` PerturbDiff checkpoints after verifying inputs, recipe and weights. Pretrain the other four models on the shared Tahoe input.

Fine-tuning updates all trainable parameters through the model's own update rules. Set every tenv optimizer group's base learning rate to `2e-4` and retain the model-specific optimizer types, schedules and alternating updates. Start fresh optimizer, scheduler and applicable EMA state. Transfer parameters with compatible meaning, including the chemical and dose adapters. Rebuild tenv lookup tables and reinitialize the dataset-specific drug-label adversary output layers in CPA and chemCPA.

Deliver checkpoints under `output/checkpoints/` and the figure `output/models_transfer_metrics.png`. Score both stages on the same tenv test groups with the eight Step 03 metrics. The `pretrain` prediction uses the selected Tahoe checkpoint directly and the `finetune` prediction uses its same-seed tenv descendant. Each metric has 12 bars, with `no_change` and `delta_mean` scored once and a `pretrain` and `finetune` pair for each model. Each model bar gives equal weight to the three seed scores and shows the three individual points.
