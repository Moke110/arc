# Stage 3 source-first viability search

Date: 2026-11-06. Scope: primary-publication search for cardiomyocyte baseline, hypoxia and hypoxia/reoxygenation chemical treatment, with CCK8 or MTT. Ranking is chemical breadth, then dose breadth, then 24 h, then human primary/iPSC > animal primary > cell line. This is a bounded search, not proof of a global optimum. No datasets were loaded or sources acquired.

## Strongest new baseline leads

**Pohjolainen et al., 2024, “Switching of hypertrophic signalling towards enhanced cardiomyocyte identity and maturity by a GATA4-targeted compound.”** Best baseline candidate found in this bounded search. The original Fig. 1E was downloaded to `/tmp/stage3-gata4-fig1.png` and visually inspected on 2026-11-06: it contains 16 color-valued chemical rows at four doses, 1, 3, 10 and 30 µM, in hiPSC-CMs after 24 h. These are the 14 novel compounds (3i-1249 through 3i-1263, excluding 3i-1257) plus 3i-1157 and 3i-1183. The additional 3i-1000 row is gray, outside the heatmap color scale, and is not counted as numerical coverage pending its interpretation. Thus 64 colored summary positions are visible, not 64 physical samples. The 0.3 µM column belongs to hiPSC panel D, not hiPSC-CM panel E; do not conflate the panels. Synthesis methods are supplied or referenced, so structure-to-SMILES conversion is plausible but not completed. Public viability evidence is a heatmap, rather than an exact numerical table; other numerical datasets are available on reasonable request, while GEO GSE186208 contains RNAseq, not viability data. Heatmap extraction would be approximate and its color limits must not be treated as exact values outside the scale. [Original article, Fig. 1, synthesis, methods and data availability](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13287-023-03623-x), [original full-resolution Fig. 1](https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1186%2Fs13287-023-03623-x/MediaObjects/13287_2023_3623_Fig1_HTML.png).

**Karhu et al., 2018, “Stem cells are the most sensitive screening tool to identify toxicity of GATA4-targeted novel small-molecule compounds.”** Eight compounds have baseline hiPSC-CM MTT after 24 h (Fig. 5a), with seven nonzero doses in the figure legend: 0.01, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10 and 30 µM; DMSO controls and mean ± SEM, n=3–4. Thus 56 drug–dose summary positions are expected, not 56 individual biological samples. The compounds are 3i-1000, 3i-1047, 3i-1051, 3i-1120, 3i-1148, 3i-1165, 3i-1228 and 3i-1229. Fig. 1 supplies structures; synthesis detail is supplementary. Primary neonatal-rat cardiomyocytes and H9c2 are also measured but must stay separate from hiPSC-CMs. Public figures support extraction; a machine-readable viability table was not verified. This is a well-defined baseline fallback or related-source extension. [Original article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00204-018-2257-1), [author-repository PDF with Fig. 5 legend](https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/23736/nbnfi-fe2019062822323.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).

## Hypoxia and H/R status

No broad, condition-exact single-source hypoxia/H/R CCK8/MTT screen was verified in this bounded search. The previously archived small sources remain candidates, not displaced merely by unrelated large screens. The existing [viability-source assessment](stage3-viability-sources.md) documents Duan 2013: SYA at three doses plus NAC at one dose, neonatal-rat primary cells, 24 h pretreatment then 3 h anoxia/6 h reoxygenation, MTT. Counts of compounds with recoverable chemical identity still require SYA disambiguation. Liu 2021 has icariin at multiple doses in baseline and H/R CCK8; broader chemical counts cannot include mechanism combinations as single-agent observations.

One fresh pure-hypoxia lead is Boccellino et al. 2021, H9c2, 24 h hypoxia, MTT Fig. 1a with EHT1864 at 20 µM and two glucose contexts. It is narrow and glucose changes the experimental background; the paper's 1400W experiment is a proliferation result and must not automatically be counted as additional MTT drug coverage. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8497098/).

## Recommended next source audit

Select the 2024 GATA4 study as the leading baseline anchor candidate (16 color-valued chemical series × four doses), then audit its chemical structures and obtain or explicitly approximate the numerical MTT values. Karhu 2018 Fig. 5a is the first similar-source candidate (eight chemicals × seven doses). Retain the oxygen-injury sources separately. All reported counts above are publication coverage, not an acquired corpus or SMILES-resolved unique-chemical count. No author contact was made.

Additional broad-screen checks: Gerö 2007 uses H2O2 oxidative injury despite a reperfusion-model rationale, so is outside exact H/R ([publisher abstract](https://www.spandidos-publications.com/10.3892/ijmm.20.5.749)). Guo 2012 screens 2,000 molecules in an IR apparatus, but its first pass pools four compounds per well and only 224 proceed individually; its phenotype must be confirmed as MTT/CCK8 before it can compete ([original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3329894/)).
