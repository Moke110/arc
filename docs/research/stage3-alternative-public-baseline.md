# Additional public baseline viability source search

Research date: 2026-11-06. Scope: sources beyond Huang 2022 and Burnett 2021, with >689 chemicals, cardiomyocyte baseline exposure and publicly downloadable numerical viability data. This bounded search did not verify another qualifying source.

## Primary-source exclusions

- **Grafton 2021, eLife 68714**: 1,280 bioactive compounds at 0.3, 1 and 3 µM for four days in human iPSC-CMs. The article distinguishes a learned structural-cardiotoxicity score from nuclear-count cell survival. The public Fig.5 workbook supplies full-library score distributions, while names and nuclear-count comparison concern selected hits; a full chemical-linked viability matrix is not established. Do not relabel structural score as cell viability or GEO RNAseq as full phenotype data. [Article](https://elifesciences.org/articles/68714), [Fig.5 workbook](https://cdn.elifesciences.org/articles/68714/elife-68714-fig5-data1-v2.xlsx).
- **Kane et al. 2013 NIH probe project, summary AID588508**: approximately 360,000 compounds, H9c2/HL-1, ATP viability measured using ATPLite. The original report specifies H2O2 oxidative stress or 2-deoxyglucose metabolic stress; Table 2 lists primary/confirmation/SAR assays in these stress contexts, without a broad unstressed baseline arm. The large public library therefore does not meet baseline eligibility. [NIH primary report](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK174883/), [assay table](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK174883/table/ml331.t2/?report=objectonly).
- **H9c2 triacylglycerol screening project, summary AID651587**: PubChem records describe Nile-red lipid accumulation with DAPI nuclear fluorescence as the cytotoxicity counter-readout. However, the documented protocol uses 1% FBS plus 200 µM oleic acid, an explicit lipid-loading experimental background. AID 743161 is only 70 compounds in SAR and cannot establish >689 baseline viability records. Do not treat primary Nile-red data as cell viability. [Primary depositor assay protocol](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioassay/743161), [project summary](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioassay/651587).
- **Development of a novel therapy for systolic heart failure (2025)**: approximately 5,000 WECC compounds screened in H9c2, but resazurin viability follows dobutamine/rolipram stimulation. This is a separate pharmacological stress background, not an unstressed baseline chemical screen. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12423297/).

The large Cell Painting resources found in the Broad public gallery predominantly use U2OS/A549 and other noncardiomyocyte lines; compound scale and public access alone do not satisfy the requested cell type. [Primary gallery inventory](https://broadinstitute.github.io/cellpainting-gallery/complete_datasets.html).

## Direct Grafton source-workbook inspection

The [public Figure 5 workbook](https://cdn.elifesciences.org/articles/68714/elife-68714-fig5-data1-v2.xlsx) was downloaded successfully (103,618 bytes) and its original Strict OOXML inspected without rewriting it. `openpyxl` did not expose worksheets because of the Strict namespace; direct ZIP/XML parsing showed four worksheets. Three `All Plates` sheets contain score arrays headed `Compound Library`, plus DMSO/bortezomib/doxorubicin columns, rather than full chemical identifiers. The fourth `Hits` sheet contains named selected compounds. These are deep-learning structural-toxicity scores, not a complete chemical-linked viability export. Library dose and duration are 0.3, 1 and 3 µM for 4 days; no 24 h interpretation is made. Raw images/models are offered through author contact in the peer-review response, not a verified public full-image release. The full-library row-to-chemical map and full-library survival/nuclear-count readouts were not recovered.
