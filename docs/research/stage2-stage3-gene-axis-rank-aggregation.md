## Fusion contract

Exact recipe strings and constants are in
`stage2/01_bases/output/gene-axis/manifest.json → recipe` and `constants`.

- **Within-source ranks only.** A source's own order of the axis genes is its
  HVG rank (where an expression matrix exists) or its DEG rank. Microarray and
  RNA-seq effect sizes are never compared directly.
- **DEG score.** `|log2FC| × -log10(padj)` with `padj` floored at `1e-300`; an NA
  `padj` (DESeq2 independent filtering) is non-significant but stays measured.
  A multi-contrast source collapses per gene by the **maximum** effect over
  contrasts and records `n_contrasts_supporting` (padj < 0.05).
- **HVG score.** Seurat-style normalized dispersion on the item's deposited log
  expression: sample variance / mean, log dispersion z-scored in 20 equal-width
  `log1p(mean)` bins, singleton bins score 1, undefined dispersion sorts last,
  `gene_index` tie-break. Deliberate difference from
  `stage2/01_bases/output/api/bases/hvg.py`: no `expm1` recovery, because the
  disease matrices are heterogeneous non-invertible log scales (voom log2
  abundance, `log2(RPKM+1)`, `log2(CPM+1)`, `log2(CP10K+1)`).
- **Unmeasured genes** get raw rank `n_measured + 1` and `rank_norm = 1.0` (the
  worst rank); they are never silently dropped.
- **Fusion.** `rank_sum = Σ_sources rank_norm`, with
  `rank_norm = rank / n_measured`; the primary order is ascending `rank_sum`
  with the frozen `gene_index` tie-break. **No weights, no quotas, no animal
  down-weighting.** `rank_sum_animal_half` (the two mouse sources at weight 0.5)
  is a reported sensitivity column, not the chosen rule.
- **Determinism.** The whole pipeline is deterministic; `--verify` rebuilds every
  tracked output digest in a temporary directory and checks every input sha256.
- **Leakage red line.** Label-free: no tenv truth file, no Heliste label is
  opened; the only tenv input is the published descriptive HVG ranking. A test
  poisons the truth path so the boundary is executable.

### 4. Inflection — an explicit "no inflection"

File: `stage2/01_bases/output/gene-axis/inflection.json`:
`"inflection": false`, `"n": null`.

- **Kneedle** online variant, `sensitivity = 1.0`: the normalized arc
  `y_norm − x_norm` of the sorted `rank_sum` curve reaches its broad, shallow
  maximum at `n = 1314`, `arc = 0.2533404287737608`. The arc is not a sharp knee:
  it is 0.236 at N=500, 0.251 at N=1,000, 0.253 at N=1,314 and 0.247 at N=2,000.
- **Second difference** of the same normalized curve, search range
  `min_n = 100` … 18,164, `minimum_curvature = 1e-6`: the largest curvature is in
  the tail at `n = 18156`, curvature `0.003506475921318475`.
- **Acceptance rule** (`parameters`): both candidates must exist, agree within
  `agreement_tolerance = 0.25` (relative), and the knee must exceed
  `minimum_knee_strength = 0.01`. The two candidates disagree by far more than
  25%, so no `N` is reported.
- **Stability:** all ten leave-one-source-out replays report
  `"inflection": false` with `n: null` (`inflection.json →
  leave_one_source_out`), so the answer is stable to dropping any single source.

The curve is steep only over its first few outlier genes and its last
mostly-unmeasured tail; the body is close to linear. **No N is forced.**

## The three #175 questions

Also pinned in `manifest.json → answers` (and re-derived by `--verify`).

1. **Do chronic-remodelling sources bring different genes than HF?** Yes. The
   two HF sources agree with each other (Jaccard **0.4255**) but the AF/HHD pair
   is nearly disjoint (**0.0043**), and the four HF×remodelling cross pairs
   average **0.0233**. Tenv-HVG overlap is also far larger with the HF sources
   (972 / 735 genes at the tenv top-2,000) than with AF/HHD (59 / 10). AF and
   HHD contribute a different, much smaller gene set; they are not a cheaper HF
   replicate.
2. **Are up and down genes rank-symmetric?** Only mildly. 8,889 genes are
   up-significant and 7,857 down-significant; the up set's median fused rank is
   **8,561** versus **9,560** for down. In the fused top-N the up share is stable
   at **56–58%** under symmetric inclusion (≈**64%** under `|log2FC|` weight),
   with 0–56 exact direction ties. The ranking is therefore mildly up-biased, not
   symmetric — a direction rule still matters.
3. **Does effect×replication agree with DEG-count ranking?** Mostly at the top,
   less in the middle. Ranking genes by `n_sources_deg_significant` (descending;
   #175's "DEG-count" risk check) agrees with the fused ranking at Spearman
   **0.8095**; top-N intersection shares are **0.616 / 0.555 / 0.4775 / 0.4477 /
   0.7604** at N = 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 (308 / 555 / 955 / 1,343 /
   3,802 genes). Agreement is strongest at the very top, weakest around N≈3,000,
   and rises again at N=5,000. The exact-tie rule matters here because source
   counts are small integers: the product breaks ties by fused rank (the build's
   row order), whereas the record's prose says "tie-break `gene_index`"; a
   re-implementation that uses the frozen `gene_index` tie-break instead gets
   Spearman 0.6791 and top-500 overlap 0.204. The tracked number is the
   product's, and it is the one #173 should cite.

## Decisions left to #173 (not made here)

- **Axis size `N`.** There is no inflection, so #173 must choose `N` from the
  tenv non-HVG curve (`tenv_non_hvg_curve.csv`) plus the Stage 3 sample-size

## Reading notes and limitations

- **No inflection does not mean no axis.** It means the fused-score curve does
  not identify an `N`; #173 must justify `N` from the flat tenv non-HVG curve and
  the downstream sample budget.
- **Ranks erase effect magnitude.** Cross-source comparability is bought at the
  price of losing how large an effect is. That is the intended trade of an
  unweighted fusion.
- **Animal sources are DEG-only and ortholog-mapped.** Mapping losses are
  recorded; GSE108940 contributes almost no significance and should not be read
  as a replication source.
- **The sources are not interchangeable.** Bulk LV tissue carries
  cell-composition shifts a hiPSC-CM monolayer does not; GSE282618 is exploratory
  at n=5 subjects; GSE128188 is 5 vs 5 donors. The per-source summary records
  this and the fusion does not pretend otherwise.
- **The tenv ranking is descriptive over all wells.** The non-HVG share bounds
  coverage of tenv variability evidence, not model trainability under the
  train-only rankings.
- **GSE137293 and LINCS-LDS-1593 are listed-only** in `source_summary.csv`
  (`supplementary_only = True`) and never enter the fusion.
