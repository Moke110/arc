# Antimuscarinic (anticholinergic) drugs in CHD / MI / PCI — evidence-based safety assessment
# 抗胆碱能（抗毒蕈碱）药物在冠心病 / 心肌梗死 / PCI 患者中的循证安全性评估

Prepared for the MIRI drug-repositioning screen (candidates include tropicamide, cyclopentolate, doxylamine, amantadine, ± β/α-blockers).
Date: 2026-10-14. Citations are inline (journal + PMID, or FDA label).

---

## 0. Scope note / 范围说明

**EN.** This report grades safety for *antimuscarinic* exposure in patients with coronary heart disease (CHD), myocardial infarction (MI), or undergoing PCI. A crucial distinction runs through everything below: **topical ophthalmic mydriatics** (tropicamide, cyclopentolate) produce a single, low systemic dose, whereas **systemic (oral/IV) antimuscarinics** produce sustained muscarinic blockade. If a screen candidate is to be *repositioned systemically*, it must be judged by the **systemic** standard, not the ophthalmic one.

**中文.** 本报告对*抗毒蕈碱*暴露在冠心病（CHD）、心肌梗死（MI）或接受 PCI 患者中的安全性进行分级。全文贯穿着一个关键区分：**局部眼用散瞳药**（托吡卡胺、环喷托酯）为单次、低全身暴露；而**全身（口服/静脉）抗毒蕈碱药**产生持续的毒蕈碱阻断。若筛选候选药拟被*全身重定位*使用，则必须按**全身用药**标准评估，而不能按眼用标准。

## 1. Pharmacology / mechanism of concern 药理学 / 机制层面的担忧

**EN.** Muscarinic M2 receptors on the sinoatrial node mediate vagal (parasympathetic) slowing of heart rate; blocking them removes vagal tone and predisposes to tachycardia (Autonomic nervous system physiology review, Am J Pharm Educ 2007; PMID:17786266; atropine label mechanism). In a coronary circulation with fixed stenoses, any drug-induced rise in heart rate increases myocardial oxygen demand and shortens diastole (the period of coronary perfusion), potentially provoking demand ischemia (2023 ESC ACS guideline, Eur Heart J 2023; PMID:37622654). Antimuscarinics also reduce vagal "protection" against ventricular arrhythmia and, for several agents, add direct QT-prolonging potential (see §3, §5). Additional anticholinergic effects (dry mouth, urinary retention, mydriasis, CNS effects, heat intolerance) are the class toxidrome.

**中文.** 窦房结上的 M2 毒蕈碱受体介导迷走（副交感）对心率的减慢；阻断该受体会消除迷走张力，易致心动过速（自主神经生理学综述，Am J Pharm Educ 2007; PMID:17786266；阿托品说明书机制）。在存在固定狭窄的冠状动脉循环中，任何药物引起的心率升高都会增加心肌耗氧并缩短舒张期（冠状动脉灌注期），可能诱发需求性缺血（2023 ESC ACS 指南，Eur Heart J 2023; PMID:37622654）。抗毒蕈碱药还削弱迷走神经对室性心律失常的保护作用，且部分药物本身具有 QT 延长潜力（见 §3、§5）。其他抗胆碱能效应（口干、尿潴留、瞳孔散大、中枢效应、不耐热）构成该类的毒性综合征。

*Coronary heart disease / 冠心病*: 心率↑ + 收缩力↑ → 心肌耗氧↑（供需失配）。
*MI / 梗死后*: 心动过速增加梗死边缘区应激，促心律失常。
*PCI / 围术期*: 迷走反射（Bezold–Jarisch 反射，下壁 MI）本以心动过缓/低血压为主——此处抗毒蕈碱药反而是**治疗**（见 §4）。

## 2. Mydriatic antimuscarinics — tropicamide (托吡卡胺) & cyclopentolate (环喷托酯)

### 2.1 Is there a real systemic risk? 是否存在真实全身风险？
**EN.** Yes, but small at standard diagnostic doses, and most documented **serious** events involve the *co-administered sympathomimetic* phenylephrine rather than the antimuscarinic itself.
- Systemic absorption occurs via nasolacrimal drainage into the highly vascular nasal/oropharyngeal mucosa; the cyclopentolate label explicitly instructs **1 drop + nasolacrimal (punctal) pressure for 2–3 min** to minimize absorption (Cyclopentolate HCl ophthalmic solution label, Bausch & Lomb; DailyMed/openFDA).
- The tropicamide label lists **tachycardia** among reported non-ocular adverse reactions (Tropicamide ophthalmic solution label, Sandoz; DailyMed/openFDA, eff. 2022-06-29).
- Old but directly relevant review: "Systemic reactions to ophthalmic drug preparations" (Med Toxicol Adverse Drug Exp 1987; PMID:3306268) surveyed systemic reactions of topical **phenylephrine and cyclopentolate** and emphasized precautions.
- Case report: acute **heart failure / tachycardia / tachypnea** in a preterm infant after **tropicamide + phenylephrine**; the authors judged phenylephrine the more likely contributor because symptoms did not recur with tropicamide alone (SAGE Open Med Case Rep 2025; PMID:40821108).
- Case report: acute **hypertension and arrhythmia** after excessive topical **10% phenylephrine** (not the antimuscarinic) (Case Rep Med 2026; PMID:41809511).
- Review: topical phenylephrine drops carry recognised serious **cardiovascular** systemic adverse events (J Cataract Refract Surg 2024; PMID:37748029).
- NSTEMI ~10 min after **fluorescein angiography** (a procedure requiring mydriasis): reported, but causality is multifactorial (dye, stress) and not attributed to the mydriatic (Front Ophthalmol 2026; PMID:42232993).

**Honest gap.** **No case report was found of tropicamide *alone* causing acute MI in an adult**, and no controlled study of mydriatics specifically in CHD/MI cohorts was identified. → Do not overstate. The signal is tachycardia/BP liability, amplified by phenylephrine co-administration and by extreme prematurity.

**中文.** 有，但在标准诊断剂量下较小；且多数**严重**事件相关的其实是同时联用的拟交感药**去氧肾上腺素**，而非抗毒蕈碱药本身。全身吸收经鼻泪管引流至血供丰富的鼻/口咽黏膜；环喷托酯说明书明确要求**单滴 + 鼻泪管（泪点）压迫 2–3 分钟**以减少吸收。托吡卡胺说明书在非眼部不良反应中列出**心动过速**。**诚实说明证据缺口**：未检索到成人中**单用托吡卡胺**致急性 MI 的病例报告，也未见专门针对 CHD/MI 人群的散瞳药对照研究。风险信号主要是心动过速/血压波动，且被去氧肾上腺素联用及早产儿极端体质放大。

### 2.2 What do labels say — contraindication or caution? 说明书是禁忌还是慎用？
**EN.** For both agents the **only** listed contraindication is **hypersensitivity** — cardiac disease is **not** a labelled contraindication.
- **Tropicamide** (Sandoz; openFDA, eff. 2022-06-29): *Contraindications* = hypersensitivity to any component. *Warnings* = topical ophthalmic use only; CNS disturbances/pediatric risk; possible psychotic reactions; transient IOP elevation. No cardiac contraindication.
- **Cyclopentolate** (Bausch & Lomb; openFDA, eff. 2022-09-29): *Contraindications* = hypersensitivity. *Warnings* = topical ophthalmic only; **infants especially prone to CNS and cardiopulmonary side effects**; anticholinergic toxidrome includes tachycardia; caution/avoid if narrow angle.

So the label posture is **not "contraindicated" but "no specific cardiac warning"** — i.e., use judgment/caution. Historical/ophthalmic teaching commonly adds "use with caution in cardiac disease" (consistent with the phenylephrine/cyclopentolate systemic-reaction review, PMID:3306268), but this is **not** a formal US-label contraindication.

**中文.** 两药说明书**唯一**列出的禁忌是**过敏**——心脏疾病**并非**标示禁忌。托吡卡胺与环喷托酯均为局部眼用、高血压/心脏病未列为禁忌。因此说明书立场是**非"禁忌"亦无专门心脏警示**——即需临床判断/慎用。传统眼科教学常附"心脏病慎用"（与 PMID:3306268 一致），但这**不是**正式美国说明书禁忌。

### 2.3 Guidance for diabetic-retinopathy (DR) screening in cardiac patients 心脏患者糖网筛查的指导
**EN.** **No specific guidance found** for mydriatic use in DR screening among cardiac patients. Practical risk-reduction, extrapolated from label labeling and the systemic-absorptive mechanism: use the **lowest effective concentration and fewest drops** (e.g., 0.5% tropicamide for fundoscopy), apply **punctal occlusion**, avoid unnecessary co-administered **phenylephrine** (the main CV offender), avoid the **2% cyclopentolate** strength in older/vulnerable patients, and monitor HR/BP/symptoms during the examination. (Basis: tropicamide & cyclopentolate labels; PMID:3306268; PMID:37748029.)

**中文.** **未检索到**针对心脏患者糖网筛查时散瞳用药的专门指南。基于说明书标示与全身吸收机制可采取的减危措施：使用**最低有效浓度、最少滴数**（如眼底检查用 0.5% 托吡卡胺）、**泪点压迫**、避免不必要的**去氧肾上腺素**联用（主要心血管不良反应来源）、避免老年人使用 **2% 环喷托酯**，并在检查期间监测心率/血压/症状。

## 3. Systemic antimuscarinics 全身抗毒蕈碱药

**EN.** Label posture for this class, verbatim:
- **Oxybutynin (ER)** (openFDA, eff. 2023-05-31): *Contraindications* = urinary retention, gastric retention/other severe decreased GI motility, uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma, hypersensitivity. **No cardiac contraindication**; warnings address angioedema, CNS effects, urinary retention. Postmarketing lists tachycardia/palpitations.
- **Solifenacin** (openFDA, eff. 2026-01-28): same contraindications; **"QT Prolongation in Patients at High Risk of QT Prolongation: … not recommended for use in patients at high risk of QT prolongation, including … known history of QT prolongation and patients taking medications known to prolong QT."**
- **Tolterodine (ER)**: same contraindications; **QT-prolongation warning** (Class IA/III antiarrhythmics interaction); postmarketing **tachycardia, palpitations**.
- **Glycopyrrolate (injection)**: contraindicated in **"unstable cardiovascular status in acute hemorrhage"** (with the classic peptic-ulcer cluster: glaucoma, obstructive uropathy, etc.). **Warning 5.5 (Tachycardia): "Increase in heart rate may occur. Use with caution in patients with coronary artery disease, congestive heart failure, cardiac arrhythmias, hypertension, or hyperthyroidism."** ← the canonical "caution in CAD" statement.
- **Hyoscyamine** (incl. atropine/scopolamine/phenobarbital combination): contraindicated in **"unstable cardiovascular status in acute hemorrhage."**
- **Dicyclomine**: contraindicated in **"unstable cardiovascular status in acute hemorrhage."**
- **Atropine sulfate (injection)**: **"CONTRAINDICATIONS: None."** (see §4).

**Summary of the class:** chronic oral antimuscarinics (OAB agents) are **not** contraindicated in CHD, but carry **tachycardia/QT** liabilities (especially solifenacin/tolterodine) and should be used at lowest dose with monitoring; GI antispasmodics carry an explicit **"unstable cardiovascular status in acute hemorrhage"** contraindication; glycopyrrolate carries an explicit CAD **caution**.

**中文.** 该类药物说明书立场（原文）：**奥昔布宁、索利那新、托特罗定**禁忌为尿潴留、胃潴留/严重胃肠动力下降、未控制的闭角型青光眼、过敏——**均无心脏禁忌**；但**索利那新、托特罗定**带 **QT 延长警示**（高风险 QT 患者"不推荐使用"），托特罗定上市后报告**心动过速/心悸**。**格隆溴铵（注射）**禁忌包括**"急性出血时血流动力学不稳定"**，并有经典警示 **5.5 心动过速**："心率可升高。在**冠状动脉疾病、充血性心力衰竭、心律失常、高血压或甲状腺功能亢进**患者中应慎用"——这是"CAD 慎用"的权威表述。**莨菪碱、双环维林**同样禁忌"急性出血时血流动力学不稳定"。**阿托品**则**"禁忌：无"**（见 §4）。

## 4. Atropine in the peri-MI / PCI setting — resolving the apparent contradiction 围 MI / PCI 期阿托品：化解表面矛盾

**EN.** Atropine is **guideline-recommended** for symptomatic bradycardia/AV block complicating acute MI (especially **inferior** MI) and during PCI, and its label lists **no contraindication**:
- 2023 ESC ACS guideline (Eur Heart J 2023; PMID:37622654) and 2018 ACC/AHA/HRS Bradycardia guideline (JACC 2019; PMID:30412709; Circulation 2019; PMID:30586772) endorse atropine for symptomatic bradycardia.
- Inferior STEMI is complicated by **vagally mediated** bradyarrhythmia/hypotension (Bezold–Jarisch reflex); a prospective double-blind RCT tested **IV atropine given just before reperfusion** during primary PCI in inferior STEMI (J Saudi Heart Assoc 2026; PMID:42305382).
- Scoping review of IV atropine dosing for symptomatic bradycardia (Circ Rep 2025; PMID:41216404).

**Why this is not a contradiction:** the *problem being treated* is pathological **bradycardia/hypotension** (with reduced coronary perfusion), and the *desired effect* is to restore rate — i.e., **therapeutic vagolysis**. Risk in MI comes not from "anticholinergic" per se but from **inappropriate tachycardia in a fixed-stenosis, demand-limited heart**. Therapy uses **titrated, monitored IV boluses in a critical-care setting** (titrate to HR/BP/symptoms per the atropine label), which is fundamentally different from **chronic systemic antimuscarinic exposure** at home. The apparent paradox therefore reflects **dose, route, acuity, indication, and monitoring**, not a class inconsistency. (Basis: atropine label "Contraindications: None"; PMID:37622654; PMID:30412709; PMID:42305382; PMID:41216404.)

**中文.** 阿托品用于急性 MI（尤其**下壁** MI）并发的症状性心动过缓/房室传导阻滞及 PCI 期间，是**指南推荐**的，且说明书**无禁忌**。**为何不矛盾**：所治疗的问题是病理性**心动过缓/低血压**（伴冠脉灌注下降），期望效应即**恢复心率**——即**治疗性迷走阻断**。MI 中的风险并非来自"抗胆碱能"本身，而来自**在固定狭窄、供需受限的心脏中出现不当的心动过速**。此时为**重症监护环境下按心率/血压/症状滴定、监测下的静脉推注**，与**居家长期全身抗毒蕈碱暴露**截然不同。看似矛盾实则反映**剂量、给药途径、急缓、适应证与监测**的差异，而非类别的自相矛盾。

## 5. Antihistamines with antimuscarinic action 具抗毒蕈碱作用的抗组胺药

**EN.**
- **Doxylamine / pyridoxine (delayed-release)** label (openFDA, eff. 2025-12-04): *Contraindications* = hypersensitivity + **MAO inhibitors**; no cardiac contraindication; main adverse reaction = somnolence.
- **Diphenhydramine** injection label: *contraindications* = neonates/premature infants, nursing mothers; *warnings* = "antihistamines should be used with **considerable caution** in patients with narrow-angle glaucoma, stenosing peptic ulcer, pyloroduodenal obstruction, symptomatic prostatic hypertrophy or bladder-neck obstruction." Adverse reactions include **hypotension, palpitations, tachycardia, extrasystoles.**
- Pro-arrhythmic evidence: **Torsades de pointes after doxylamine overdose** (HeartRhythm Case Rep 2026; PMID:41732465); **fetal PVC/PAC that resolved after maternal doxylamine discontinuation** (Cureus 2025; PMID:41552103). First-generation H1-antihistamines carry **exposure-dependent QT/TdP** liability via cardiac ion-channel inhibition (Pharmaceuticals 2026; PMID:42198344).

**Clinical read-across:** first-generation antihistamines (doxylamine, diphenhydramine, chlorpheniramine) are **high on anticholinergic-burden scales** (see §6) and have a **dose/exposure-dependent arrhythmia signal**; in CHD/MI they should be used **sparingly, short-term, at low dose**, with QT caution (especially combined with other QT-prolongers).

**中文.** **多西拉敏/吡哆醇**说明书禁忌仅为过敏与 **MAOI**，无心脏禁忌，主要不良反应为嗜睡。**苯海拉明**注射剂禁忌为新生儿/早产儿、哺乳期；警示"在闭角型青光眼、幽门十二指肠梗阻、症状性前列腺肥大或膀胱颈梗阻患者中**须格外慎用**"；不良反应包括低血压、心悸、心动过速、早搏。致心律失常证据：**多西拉敏过量致尖端扭转性室速**（PMID:41732465）；**停用母体多西拉敏后胎儿室早/房早消失**（PMID:41552103）；第一代 H1 抗组胺药存在**暴露依赖的 QT/TdP 风险**（PMID:42198344）。在 CHD/MI 中应**低剂量、短期、谨慎**使用，尤其注意 QT（与其他 QT 延长药联用时）。

## 6. Anticholinergic burden & QT 抗胆碱能负荷与 QT

**EN.** "Anticholinergic burden" (ACB) is the cumulative anticholinergic effect of all co-prescribed drugs; several validated scales exist (systematic review, J Am Geriatr Soc 2026; PMID:41730788). Relevance to this cohort:
- In hospitalised patients with **cardiovascular disease**, **40.4%** had a *high* anticholinergic burden on admission (falling to 22.6% after pharmacist-led review) — i.e., cardiac patients frequently carry a heavy anticholinergic load (INFAR study, Basic Clin Pharmacol Toxicol 2026; PMID:41345711).
- A UK Biobank target-trial emulation found **anticholinergic vs non-anticholinergic antidepressants** differed in long-term cardiovascular-event risk, suggesting anticholinergic burden is not cardiovasculary neutral (Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf 2026; PMID:42449494).
- **QT:** among the agents here, **solifenacin** and **tolterodine** carry explicit QT warnings; **doxylamine/diphenhydramine** have arrhythmia/TdP signals; on the contrary **atropine's label warns of no QT issue but of tachycardia**.

**Practical implication:** in CHD/MI/PCI, **avoid stacking** antimuscarinics (e.g., a mydriatic *plus* a systemic antimuscarinic *plus* a sedating antihistamine), and screen for other QT-prolongers.

**中文.** "抗胆碱能负荷"（ACB）是全部合用药物抗胆碱能效应的累积；已有多个经验证的量表（J Am Geriatr Soc 2026; PMID:41730788）。与该人群相关：在**心血管疾病**住院患者中，**40.4%** 入院时属于**高**抗胆碱能负荷（药师审校后降至 22.6%）（INFAR 研究，PMID:41345711）；英国生物样本库的目标试验模拟提示**抗胆碱能 vs 非抗胆碱能抗抑郁药**的长期心血管事件风险存在差异（PMID:42449494）。**QT**方面：本清单中**索利那新、托特罗定**有明确 QT 警示；**多西拉敏/苯海拉明**有致心律失常/TdP 信号。实用建议：CHD/MI/PCI 患者应**避免叠加**抗毒蕈碱药，并排查其他 QT 延长药。

## 7. Bottom line — graded recommendation table 结论——分级推荐表

Legend: ✅ acceptable · ⚠️ caution/monitoring · ⛔ avoid/contraindicated · 🎯 indicated (therapeutic)

| Subclass / agent | CHD (stable) | Acute MI / PCI | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Topical mydriatics — tropicamide 0.5%/1%, cyclopentolate 0.5%/1%** | ✅ acceptable (lowest dose, punctal occlusion) | ⚠️ caution (single diagnostic dose OK; avoid phenylephrine co-use; monitor HR/BP) | Labels: only hypersensitivity contraindicated; tachycardia in non-ocular ADRs (tropicamide label; cyclopentolate label; PMID:3306268) |
| **Phenylephrine (added mydriatic, NOT antimuscarinic)** | ⚠️ caution | ⛔ avoid if unstable | PMID:37748029; PMID:41809511; PMID:40821108 |
| **Atropine IV — symptomatic bradycardia/AV block** | 🎯 indicated (titrated) | 🎯 indicated (titrated, monitored) | Label "Contraindications: None"; ESC 2023 PMID:37622654; ACC/AHA/HRS 2019 PMID:30412709; PMID:42305382; PMID:41216404 |
| **Glycopyrrolate (IV)** | ⚠️ caution (explicit CAD/CHF/arrhythmia caution) | ⚠️ short-term acceptable for bradycardia | Label 5.5 "use with caution in CAD, CHF, arrhythmias, hypertension, hyperthyroidism" |
| **OAB antimuscarinics — oxybutynin, tolterodine, solifenacin** | ⚠️ caution (lowest dose; prefer agents w/o QT issue; consider mirabegron†) | ⚠️ avoid unless clearly needed | Oxybutynin label: no cardiac CI; Solifenacin & Tolterodine labels: QT warnings; tolterodine postmarketing tachycardia |
| **Atropine/hyoscyamine/dicyclomine (GI/antispasmodic)** | ⚠️ caution | ⛔ avoid in "unstable cardiovascular status in acute hemorrhage" | Hyoscyamine & dicyclomine labels: explicit CI; glycopyrrolate label: same |
| **First-gen antihistamines — doxylamine, diphenhydramine** | ⚠️ caution (short-term, low dose) | ⚠️/⛔ avoid chronic/sedating dosing; QT caution | Doxylamine label (no cardiac CI); diphenhydramine label warnings; PMID:41732465; PMID:41552103; PMID:42198344 |
| **Amantadine (weak anticholinergic)** | ⚠️ caution | ⚠️ caution | Label: overdose → arrhythmia/tachycardia/hypertension (anticholinergic); CHF as adverse reaction; possible net CV benefit in parkinsonism (PMID:40035570) |
| **High cumulative anticholinergic burden (any combination)** | ⛔ avoid stacking; deprescribe | ⛔ avoid | PMID:41345711; PMID:42449494; PMID:41730788 |
| **β-blockers** (CHD/MI) | ✅ guideline therapy (protective) | ✅ guideline therapy | ESC 2023 PMID:37622654 |
| **α-blockers (e.g., doxazosin)** | ⚠️ first-dose hypotension | ⚠️ caution | general pharmacology; out of antimuscarinic scope |

†Mirabegron (β3-agonist) avoids antimuscarinic effects but can raise blood pressure — not automatically safer in CHD.

**One-line verdict / 一句话结论:** For **stable CHD**, a single diagnostic **topical mydriatic** dose is acceptable with punctal occlusion and phenylephrine avoidance ⚠️; **titrated IV atropine is indicated** for bradycardia in MI/PCI 🎯; **chronic systemic antimuscarinics and first-generation antihistamines** are the agents that warrant real caution/monitoring ⚠️, and **any antimuscarinic in "unstable cardiovascular status" or stacked anticholinergic burden should be avoided** ⛔. If any screened candidate is meant for **systemic** repositioning, judge it by the systemic-antimuscarinic standard, not the ophthalmic one.

## 8. Evidence gaps (stated honestly) 证据缺口（诚实说明）

1. **No RCT** of mydriatics (tropicamide/cyclopentolate) in CHD/MI cohorts. 未见相关 RCT。
2. **No case report found** of tropicamide-only acute MI in adults. 未见成人单用托吡卡胺致急性 MI 的病例报告。
3. **No specific guidance found** for mydriatic use during DR screening in cardiac patients. 未见针对心脏患者糖网筛查散瞳用药的专门指南。
4. Causal evidence that **antimuscarinics cause MI** is observational/indirect; the mechanistic concern (tachycardia, QT, vagal withdrawal) is stronger than the direct epidemiologic evidence. 抗毒蕈碱药致 MI 的证据为观察性/间接；机制层面的担忧强于直接的流行病学证据。
